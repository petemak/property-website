- Location: on the border between Gacuriro and Kagugu (Kinyinya sector, Gasabo district), Kigali. The exact address is only shared with a guest after their booking request is approved; the public site shows an approximate area only.
- Price: USD 70 per night. Cleaning fee and minimum-stay length are set by the owner in the admin panel.
- Car add-on: 2019 Kia Sorento, USD 45/day, collected on site.
- The public contact email address and the address(es) that receive new-request alerts are configurable by the owner in the admin Settings screen; the outbound mail sender is configured via backend environment variables (no address hardcoded).
